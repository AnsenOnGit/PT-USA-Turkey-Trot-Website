Run, walk, or host your own trot

Join PEER Teens in supporting life-giving projects of the PEER Servants partners. If you can attend, please fill out the registration form and choose your favorite cause to support.

Can't make it in person? Gather friends and host your own trot at a different time and place. Just fill out the same form and note that you'll be hosting your own trot.

How to donate

Support a runner, walker, or trotter at the event:

  1. Go to our crowdfunding page to see the projects bringing food & water security to our global family, and pick one to support.
  2. Click the picture of your favorite project, or the word “details,” to read more about it.
  3. Click “Donate now.” Where it asks “Which event are you supporting,” choose “Turkey Trot.” Under “Trotter/Walker,” you can type the name of the participant you're supporting (optional).

About PEER Teens

PEER Teens is a group of young people who want to do amazing good through outreach projects. We're part of a larger network of teens around the world and operate under the umbrella of PEER Servants.
