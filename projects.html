How to direct your donation

  1. Open the 2026 Turkey Trot Online Donation Page and enter your gift.
  2. At the end of the form, find the dropdown under “Please direct my 2026 Turkey Trot donation to...”
  3. Select the project you want to support. The project names below match the choices in the donation form.

Your donation will be added to the fundraising total at the end of each day. The project and overall totals on the event website are updated daily.

The Bloomerang form accepts credit cards, bank accounts, and Google Pay. A project-specific button below opens the same donation form; it does not preselect the project, so please choose the matching project in the dropdown.

Project 1 · Philippines

Solarized Clean Water Supply

CCT needs 22 grants of $87 each to install a hybrid solar-powered pumping system that will provide reliable access to clean water for 220 students and nearby tribal families. By making the existing deep well fully operational, this project will help improve sanitation, protect children's health, and provide a sustainable water supply for the school and the surrounding community.

Project goal: $1,914

Project 3 · Philippines

FAITH Springs - Community Water Infrastructure

CCT needs 20 grants of $100 each to support the FAITH Springs project that will establish a communal water pump and distribution system supporting vegetable gardens in Lupang Pangako. This project will benefit 186 community residents, including vulnerable families, rescued street children and youth, and seniors, by providing more reliable water access for year-round food production. With a sustainable water supply, families can continue growing nutritious vegetables, improve household food security, and build on the success of their first harvest.

Project goal: $2,000

Another way to give: RoundUp

RoundUp lets you round eligible Visa or Mastercard purchases up to the next dollar and donate the difference to PEER Servants. The roundup amounts are combined into one charge each month. All RoundUp funds support PEER Servants' Food/Water Security partners.

Sign-up takes less than a minute through RoundUp.org, an approved Visa and Mastercard financial partner.
