How to donate

To support a runner or walker:

  1. Go to our crowdfunding page and look at the projects. Pick one you'd like to support.
  2. Click the project's picture or the word "details" to read more about it.
  3. Click "Donate now." Where it asks "Which event are you supporting," choose "Turkey Trot." Under "Trotter/Walker" you can type the name of the person you're supporting (optional).

Who we are

About PEER Teens

PEER Teens is a group of young people who want to do good through outreach projects. We're part of a larger network of teens around the world, under PEER Servants.

On a weekend when many of us celebrate having plenty, we want to respond with compassion and action to food insecurity around the world. Thank you so much for your support!
